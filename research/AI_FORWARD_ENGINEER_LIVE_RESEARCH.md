# AI Forward Engineer — Live Market Research (2026-05-27)

## 🔥 Key Findings from Exa Search

### What is a Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)?
An engineer who **embeds with customers**, ships custom AI deployments, and owns the loop between product and revenue. Not just coding — bridging the gap between AI models and real customer environments.
